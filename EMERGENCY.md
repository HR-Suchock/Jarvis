# Jarvis Emergency plan (v1.39.0)

Phone is the device you carry, so emergency features live on the phone. The PC can watch and help.

## Phone (Settings > Emergency, or say "emergency" / "SOS")
- Loud siren and survival whistle (3 blasts) on the alarm stream at full volume, even when silent. Auto-stops after 5 min. The same siren is used by **Find my phone**.
- SOS flashlight and Morse message flashing (Camera torch). Single message ends itself; repeat stops after 10 min.
- Screen strobe, emergency card (blood group, allergies, contact), call 999 / 112 buttons.
- My location (GPS, needs Location permission) with map and a ready-to-send SMS (you tap send).
- Survival guide: signalling, water, shelter, flood, fire, earthquake, first aid, battery.
- All offline, no AI tokens.

## PC (Device Link > Phone controls)
- PHONE LOCATION (position + battery, opens the map), LOUD SIREN, WHISTLE, SOS FLASHLIGHT, STOP ALL SIGNALS, RING PHONE.
- Needs: Device Link on, "Allow remote control" on, Location allowed on the phone.
- Say "where is my phone" on the PC.

## Not tested on real hardware
Torch timing, siren loudness, GPS fix speed, remote location over Device Link.
