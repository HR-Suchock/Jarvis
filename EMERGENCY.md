# Emergency mode (v1.40.0)

Phone only. Say "emergency", use the Emergency chip, or add the "Jarvis emergency" home-screen widget.

- Red screen. SIREN, WHISTLE, SOS LIGHT, STROBE, MORSE and LOCATION are toggles: tap to start, tap again to stop.
- A small Jarvis talks you through emergencies (emergency questions only). Offline it uses the built-in Survival Book (no AI tokens); online with a key it reasons about the exact situation.
- Survival Book: chapters, steps, do-not lists, tips. Fully offline.
- Location tracking, map, ready-to-send text, emergency card, battery saver.
- Widget: SIREN and SOS LIGHT toggles plus OPEN.
- Not tested on real hardware: siren volume, torch, GPS, widget. Always call 999 / 112 first.
